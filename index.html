Local-First & Privacy-Focused

Privacy Policy for Hydrelune

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Overview

Your privacy is paramount to us. Hydrelune is engineered as a local-first application designed to respect and protect your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is managed when you use our application.

Summary: Hydrelune does not require account registration, does not collect personal data, and does not transmit your workout or health logs to external cloud servers.

2. Information Collection and Storage

Hydrelune operates strictly on an on-device architecture. All user data generated within the app—including workout templates, session histories, personal bests, weight tracking, water intake, and sleep metrics—is stored exclusively on your local device.

  • No Account Creation: You can use all features of Hydrelune without registering an account or providing personal credentials.
  • No Data Transmission: We do not host central servers or remote databases to harvest, upload, or sync your activity logs.

3. Data Control and Retention

Because all data remains strictly on your local physical device, you retain absolute ownership and control over your records at all times:

  • Data Deletion: You may clear your records or delete specific entries directly inside the application.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling Hydrelune or clearing application data through your device settings will permanently remove all stored history and logs. We cannot restore deleted data as we do not keep remote backups.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Hydrelune does not integrate third-party tracking software, advertising frameworks, or telemetry analytics that monitor your behavior, track your location, or profile your fitness habits across other websites or applications.

5. Children's Privacy

Hydrelune does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). Since our software does not collect personal data from any user, it fully complies with standard privacy frameworks including COPPA and GDPR.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory adjustments. Any modifications will be posted within the app or on the official app store store listing page with an updated "Last updated" date.
